# Report boundaries

Reports may state computational evidence, missing evidence, and suggested research validation. They must not state that a candidate is a confirmed neoantigen, that a treatment will work, that immune therapy is ineffective, or that a clinical resistance diagnosis has been made.

Patient reports retain major findings, uncertainty, suggested validation and research boundaries. Technical reports retain tool/reference provenance, integrity checks, evidence domains, conflicts, dual-ranking differences, reason codes and missing-evidence states.
